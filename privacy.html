2. Whose data it is

  • Businesses and their team. For the account details of a business owner and the people they invite, we decide how the data is used, as described here.
  • Callers. When you call a business that uses utter, that business decides why your call is handled and what happens to your details. We process caller data on the business’s behalf and on its instructions. Questions about how a particular business uses your details are best sent to that business; we will help it answer.
  • Google user data. When a business owner connects Google Sheets or Google Calendar, we access only what they choose, only to provide the features they turned on. Section 5 explains this in full.

3. What we collect

From business owners and their team

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, and the business’s name, address, opening hours, languages and time zone.
  • What the owner tells utter about the business: menus, services, prices, stock, answers to common questions, and messages exchanged with utter while setting up the agent.
  • The people the owner invites (name and phone number) and whose calendar is whose.
  • Billing records: the minutes used, the packs bought, and payment ids and amounts. Card and bank details are handled by our payment provider and are not stored by us.
  • A record of important actions in the account (for example, connecting or disconnecting an integration), kept for security.

From callers

  • The caller’s phone number and the time and length of the call.
  • The call audio, which is turned into text as the call happens so the agent can answer. Where the business records calls, the recording is stored. The agent tells callers at the start of the call when it is recorded.
  • The transcript of the call and what the caller asked for: an order, a booking, a question, and the name and details the caller gives for it.

From Google, when a business owner connects it

  • Google Sheets: the contents of the one spreadsheet the owner picks in Google’s file picker, limited to the columns the owner matches to fields. Columns the owner skips are not stored.
  • Google Calendar: the list of the owner’s calendars (names and ids), and whether a calendar is free or busy at a given time. We do not read the title, description, attendees or any other detail of the owner’s events.

From visitors to utter.sh

The website has no analytics or advertising trackers. It remembers the business type and language you chose in the demo in your browser’s local storage, which never leaves your device. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. “Book a demo” opens Calendly, which has its own privacy policy.

4. How we use it

  • To answer the business’s calls: understand the caller, answer from what the business has told utter, and take orders, bookings and messages.
  • To show the business its calls, orders, bookings and analytics, and to send the confirmations the business has turned on.
  • To run, secure and bill the service, and to fix problems.
  • To contact business owners about their account.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Google user data

utter’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The use of information received from Google Workspace APIs will also adhere to the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy. utter does not use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

How we store it

  • Google’s access and refresh tokens are encrypted before they are stored and are only opened by utter’s servers to call Google for the owner’s business.
  • Rows from the picked spreadsheet are stored as the business’s items (name, price, stock and the other columns the owner matched), with the spreadsheet’s id and name, the tab’s name and how its columns map. utter checks the sheet every 15 minutes, or when the owner asks, if the owner chose that.
  • For Calendar, utter stores the calendar ids and names the owner assigned, and for each booking it put in a calendar, the event’s id and time. Free and busy times are used during the call and are not stored.
  • A booking event in the owner’s calendar contains the service, the caller’s name and the caller’s phone number.

Who can see it

Only the members of the business’s utter workspace see the business’s Google-derived data, and each business’s data is kept apart from every other business’s. The business’s voice agent uses it to answer that business’s callers, which means a caller can hear an item’s name, price or availability, or a free time. utter staff access it only when needed to support the business at its request, for security, or to comply with the law. Sharing

We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or let anyone read it except as described here. We transfer it only to the service providers that run utter for us (section 7), only to provide the features the owner uses, under contracts that keep it confidential.

Disconnecting and deletion

  • The owner can disconnect Google Sheets or Google Calendar at any time in Settings → Integrations. utter then revokes its access with Google and deletes the stored tokens, the sheet settings, and the calendar choices.
  • Items that came from the sheet stay in the owner’s utter list as the owner’s own, so the agent can keep answering; the owner can delete them at any time. TODO: decide whether disconnect should also offer to delete them (recommended)
  • Booking events already in the owner’s calendar stay there; they are the owner’s. The record of which event belongs to which booking is deleted with the booking.
  • You can also remove utter’s access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. utter then stops reading and writing, and asks the owner to sign in again.
  • To have all of a business’s data deleted, write to the address in section 13.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Google tokens and phone-carrier credentials are encrypted at rest with keys held separately from the database. Each business’s data is kept apart at the database level, and access is limited to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Business owners can see, change, export and delete their business’s data in the app, disconnect integrations, and ask us to delete the account.
  • Callers can ask the business they called, or us, to access, correct or delete their details. A caller can say “don’t record” at the start of a call.
